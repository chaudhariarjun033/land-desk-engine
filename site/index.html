Questions about selling land

How do you decide what to offer for my land?

We review recent land sales nearby, zoning, size and shape, road access, utilities, flood zone, and what it would cost to make the property buildable. Then we send a written offer.

Do I pay a commission to sell my land to you?

No. There is no agent commission or listing fee. Your written offer lists every closing cost and who pays it.

How did you get my information?

Your name and mailing address come from public property records published by county appraisal and assessor offices. To reach you by email or phone, we use a contact-data service that compiles publicly available information. We never sell your information, and if you ask us to stop contacting you, we will.

Can I sell land I inherited?

Yes. Inherited land is common. The title company or closing attorney will confirm what's needed to transfer it, such as probate or an heirship document.

Do I need to live near the land to sell it?

No. The title company or closing attorney can arrange signing near you, by mobile notary, or online where your state allows it, and sends your proceeds by wire or check.

Will anyone visit the property?

Usually, yes. Before closing, we or a builder we work with will walk the land, and a surveyor may mark the corners. You don't need to be there, and we'll let you know before anyone goes out.

What if I owe back taxes or there's a lien?

That's common. Overdue taxes and most liens can be paid off from the sale at closing, as long as the sale price covers them. Tell us up front and we'll account for it in the offer.

Can I say no?

Yes. An offer is only an offer. If you'd like us to stop contacting you, tell us once and we will.
