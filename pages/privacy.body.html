Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

Chaudhari Investments LLC ("we," "us") buys land. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. Questions: land@ccinvgroup.com.

Information we collect

  • What you send us. Your name, email, phone number, mailing address, and details about your property when you use our form or email us.
  • Public property records. Owner names, mailing addresses, parcel numbers, and property details published by county appraisal, assessor, tax, and clerk offices.
  • Contact data from service providers. To reach property owners by email or phone, we use a contact-data service that compiles publicly available information.
  • Technical data. When you submit our form, our server records your browser type and a scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address, used only to stop spam and abuse. We do not use advertising trackers, analytics cookies, or tracking pixels on this site. Our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

How we use it

  • To evaluate your property and prepare a written offer.
  • To contact you about your property by email, and by phone only if you gave us your number and permission.
  • To complete a purchase, including title, survey, and closing.
  • To keep a record of people who asked us not to contact them, so we don't.
  • To prevent spam and fraud, and to meet legal and tax obligations.

Who we share it with

We share information only as needed:

  • The title company or closing attorney handling a sale.
  • If we assign our purchase contract, the buyer who takes the assignment. We disclose any intent to assign in writing before you sign.
  • Service providers that run our business for us, such as website hosting, email, and contact-data services. They may use the information only to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

We never sell your information and never share it with others for their own marketing.

Your choices

  • Stop hearing from us. Reply "no" to any email, or write to land@ccinvgroup.com. We'll stop and keep only your email address or phone number on a do-not-contact list so it stays that way.
  • See, correct, or delete your information. Email land@ccinvgroup.com. We'll respond within 30 days. We may keep records the law requires us to keep, such as closing documents.
  • State privacy rights. Some states give residents additional rights over their personal information. Email us and we'll honor any request your state's law provides.

How long we keep it

We keep property and contact information while we're evaluating or buying a property, and for as long as tax and real estate record-keeping rules require. Information about properties we don't buy is deleted when it's no longer useful, or sooner if you ask.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections and access controls. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children

This site is for property owners and is not directed to anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
